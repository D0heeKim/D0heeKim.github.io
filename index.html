Research

01 · Computational Biology

Efficient Single-Cell Foundation Models

Developing biologically informed methods for efficient representation learning. My first-author work, AdaGeneBudget, uses cell-adaptive gene-token allocation to reduce inference cost in pretrained models, achieving up to 4.63× higher embedding-extraction throughput in evaluated settings.

02 · Multimodal Reliability

Hallucination & VLA Robustness

Investigating hallucination in large vision-language models and the robustness of vision-language-action systems to visual perturbations and language-based attacks through controlled experiments.

03 · Single-Cell & Spatial Omics

Generalization and Scientific Identifiability

Studying the interpretation of biological perturbation responses and generalization in H&E-to-spatial-transcriptomics prediction, including patient-disjoint evaluation and hierarchical error analysis.

About

I am a graduate researcher at the AGILE Lab, Ewha Womans University, advised by Prof. Uiwon Hwang. I earned a dual B.S. in Mechanical & Biomedical Engineering and Computer Science & Engineering from Ewha, and studied abroad at the University of Waterloo and Ritsumeikan University.

Previously, I worked on bioinformatics visualization tools at the Ewha Research Center for Systems Biology. I am interested in reliable computational methods and AI-assisted scientific research workflows.
